Terms of Use, Safety Notice & Credits

Last updated 19/11/2026 · Applies to Rōpū Mapi (app.ropumapi.convoy) offered by Aadized.

Safety first: Rōpū Mapi only provides guidance. Always obey posted road signs, traffic signals, road rules, and directions from police or road workers—even if the app displays or announces something different.

1. Driving safely is always your responsibility

  • You are responsible for driving safely and legally at all times.
  • Speed limits shown in the app can be wrong, missing, or out of date. They come from OpenStreetMap and other open data, not from the official speed limit register. Use them only as a reference: the posted road sign is the legal speed limit.
  • Set your destination before you drive. Use a secure phone mount and do not hold your phone. New Zealand law restricts mobile phone use while driving—let a passenger operate the app.
  • A suggested route can include roads that are closed, unsealed, private, flooded, unsafe, or unsuitable for your vehicle. Check real-world conditions and use your own judgement.
  • Voice prompts, turn alerts, arrival detection, and warnings can be late, early, cut off, or missing.
  • Convoy driving: Drive at your own safe pace. Never speed or take risks to catch up. Other members' positions and gap distances can be delayed or inaccurate.
  • Do not rely on the app in an emergency—call 111 (in New Zealand) or your local emergency number.

2. Times, traffic, and road information are estimates

  • Travel and arrival times are estimates calculated from road data, speeds measured on earlier drives, and estimates for roads without speed data. Actual travel times vary.
  • The app has no live traffic congestion data. Road closures, roadworks, and delays come from NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi's state highway feed when available; it covers state highways only, can be incomplete or delayed, and is not independently verified by us.
  • Weather, temperature, hazard notices, places, opening details, and walking-track information come from third-party open sources and may be inaccurate or out of date.

3. Privacy

Rōpū Mapi has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Full details of how location, on-device storage, convoys, and deletable problem reports work are set out in the Rōpū Mapi Privacy Policy.
