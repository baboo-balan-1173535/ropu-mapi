Rōpū Mapi Privacy Policy

Last updated 28/11/2026 · Applies to Rōpū Mapi for Android (app.ropumapi.convoy) installed from Google Play. Offered by Aadized. The same policy is shown inside the app.

Rōpū Mapi is a free navigation app built in New Zealand for people travelling together. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. This page explains what the app does with your information, who it goes to, and how long it is kept.

1. Your location

The app uses your phone's precise location to show where you are, plan routes, and give turn-by-turn directions. When you start navigating, it keeps using your location while navigation runs, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. Android shows a notification for as long as this happens. It stops when navigation ends.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, your last known location (so the map opens where you are), and your last eight destinations.
  • Map files for areas you have viewed and routes you plan, so the map keeps working where there is no signal. They take up to the size you choose under Menu → Map & Discovery (100, 250, or 500 MB). The oldest are removed first, and you can clear them there at any time.
  • The route of your last trip, for up to three days, so the same trip can be planned without signal.
  • Speeds measured on your own drives, used to improve travel time estimates.
  • A diagnostic log of app events, which includes your positions during drives. It keeps roughly the last 300 entries and leaves your phone only if you send it with a problem report (see below).
  • A random number made by the app the first time you send a problem report. It identifies this phone's reports, not you, so that you can delete them (see below).

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Problem reports (optional & deletable)

If you choose Menu → Report a problem and tap Send, the app sends the following to the app's server:

  • your note;
  • any screenshot or other picture you choose to add (the app sees only the picture you pick, not your photo library);
  • unless you untick it, the app's diagnostic log, which includes your positions during recent drives, app events, and your device model;
  • the app's build, language and Android version, and the random number described above.

The server (hosted on Render) does not keep a copy. It stores the report in the developer's private GitHub repository. GitHub is run by GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company, and its privacy statement applies. Only the developer can see the report, and it is used only to fix problems. The random number is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that can't be turned back into the number. If your phone is offline, the report waits on the phone and is sent when it can be.

  • Deleting your reports: tap Delete my reports on the Report a problem screen (from version 1.0.10013 of the app; on older versions, email the contact below). Every report sent from this phone is deleted (note, picture, and log), along with any report still waiting to be sent.
  • How long reports are kept: 90 days at most. A weekly job deletes older reports.

Email instead opens your email app (or another app you pick) with your note and the log attached. It is only sent if you send it.

4. Convoys

Only when you create or join a convoy does the app send your chosen callsign, live position, speed, direction, convoy name, route, destination name, and any pit-stop proposals or votes to our convoy server (hosted on Render), which passes them on to the other members of that convoy.

  • The server keeps convoy information in memory only, with no database. For each member it holds the latest position and about the last four minutes of positions, which it needs to tell the others when someone has stopped.
  • The convoy's leader can remove a member. The removed member stops receiving the convoy's information and the others stop seeing theirs.
  • A member's information is removed 15 seconds after they leave or lose connection. A convoy with nobody left in it is deleted at the server's next hourly clean-up.
  • The server's log records callsigns, convoy codes, and convoy names when people create, join, and leave a convoy, the names of proposed pit stops, and the internet address of a device that makes too many wrong attempts to join a convoy. It does not log positions. Render keeps these logs for a limited time under its own policy.

Other members of your convoy see your callsign and position while you are in it. Only share a convoy code with people you want to travel with.

5. Online services the app uses

To search, plan routes, draw the map, and show road and weather information, the app sends the details each request needs to the services below. They receive your phone's internet (IP) address, as with any online request, and their own privacy policies apply. We don't receive copies of these requests.

Purpose Service What is sent
Routes & road checks Project OSRM demo server; FOSSGIS e.V. routing servers (OSRM, Valhalla) Your start point, stops, and destination; your direction of travel when re-routing; the shape of a route
Live traffic on car routes TomTom, through the Rōpū Mapi server Your start point and destination (car trips of 3 km or more, when planning and when re-routing) and your direction of travel. Our server sends them to TomTom without anything that identifies you, keeps the answer for 3 minutes so nearby trips can share it, and does not log positions.
Search Photon (komoot); Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation); TomTom, through the Rōpū Mapi server, only when those find no match What you type, and the map position used to rank results. For TomTom, our server sends only the search words and that position, without anything that identifies you, keeps the answer for an hour so the same search nearby can reuse it, and does not log searches or positions.
Place names & outlines Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) A position, or the map reference of a place you picked (to find its outline and entrances)
Places along a route, and entrances of large places Overpass API servers The area around your route or destination
Weather Open-Meteo A position
Map images OpenFreeMap; Esri or OpenTopoMap if you choose the satellite or terrain map The parts of the map you look at, and the area along a route you plan (fetched ahead so it works without signal)
Road closures & roadworks NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi Nothing about you: the app downloads the whole state highway list

Some screens have links that open Google Maps or a web search in another app. Those apps' policies apply once you tap them.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information, and the app shows no advertising.
  • We don't build a profile of you or track you across other apps.
  • The app has no account and doesn't ask for your real name, email address, or phone number. The only name it uses is the convoy callsign you choose, which can be anything.
  • This version of the app doesn't use your camera, microphone, or contacts. It doesn't read your photos, apart from a picture you pick yourself to add to a problem report.
  • The Google Play version only connects over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Your rights (Privacy Act 2020)

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Most of your information never reaches us, convoy information is held only for a short time, and you can delete your problem reports from the app yourself. Please get in touch with any question or request.

8. Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the app asks you to accept its updated terms.

For privacy questions, data requests, and problem reports, email support@aadized.com.
